What does a normal domestic clean involve?

A regular clean generally concentrates on accessible surfaces in rooms that are used most: kitchen worktops and sink, bathroom fixtures, floors, and general dusting and tidying. Heavy oven cleaning, deep stain removal, post-building-work debris or moving-out requirements are different scopes and may need extra time or specialist equipment.

When comparing two quotes, check the visit duration and scope rather than relying on a headline hourly figure. A quote for two hours is not automatically cheaper or better value than a quote for three hours if the work required can't be completed properly.

Weekly, fortnightly, monthly or one-off?

Weekly: useful when kitchens, bathrooms and busy family rooms need predictable upkeep. More frequent maintenance may prevent tasks accumulating between visits.

Fortnightly: useful when everyday basic tidying is manageable but a regular deeper reset still helps. This is a common starting point for a household trying cleaning help for the first time.

Monthly: can suit less heavily used homes, or complement your own regular cleaning. You may need a longer or more focused visit if significant tasks build up.

One-off: suitable for trying a service, catching up before an event or an occasional reset. A standard one-off clean is not the same as a full end-of-tenancy or specialised deep clean.
