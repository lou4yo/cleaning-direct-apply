Questions homeowners ask before requesting a cleaner

Are you accepting bookings in KT10, KT11, KT13 or KT22?

Not yet. Cleaning.direct is collecting non-binding interest while local independent cleaners are recruited and appropriately checked. A submitted enquiry does not reserve an appointment.

Do I need to open an account to see a price?

The first-party quote experience is undergoing verification; we cannot yet promise a passwordless submission or a successfully saved enquiry.

How are cleaners selected?

The marketplace intends to connect households with independent self-employed cleaners who complete the applicable onboarding and legal identity/right-to-work steps. Applicants are not automatically approved, and we do not advertise any cleaner as verified before checks are complete.

Can I request a one-off clean instead of weekly?

Yes. The calculator compares one-off, monthly, fortnightly and weekly indicative prices. No option constitutes an appointment or a binding quote.

Who can contact me after I save my enquiry?

If an enquiry is successfully saved on the official website, Cleaning.direct may contact you about that enquiry under the contact acknowledgement shown there. Marketing choices must remain separate.
